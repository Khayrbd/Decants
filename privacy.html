Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains what information KHAYR collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, message us on WhatsApp: +8801569126359.

What we collect when you order

  • Your name, phone number, delivery address, delivery area and, if you choose to give it, your email address.
  • The perfumes, sizes and prices in your order, the order total and the delivery charge.
  • Whether you sent the order with “Order on WhatsApp” or “Copy Full Order”.

We use this only to confirm, prepare and deliver your order, and to contact you about it.

Payments

This website does not take card or account details. You pay by bKash or bank transfer yourself, using the payment details we send you on WhatsApp, and send us the payment screenshot or transaction ID.

Where your order is kept

  • Our order database (Supabase), so we can see and confirm orders. Only the KHAYR admin can read it, after logging in with a password and an authenticator code.
  • Our private Google Sheet, which we use to manage orders.
  • WhatsApp, when you send the order message to us there.

Information saved on your own phone or computer

Your cart, your payment choice and the details you type in the order form are saved in your own browser, so you do not have to type them again. They stay on your device. You can remove them by clearing this website’s data in your browser.

Advertising measurement (Meta Pixel and Conversions API)

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram. To understand which ads bring customers, this website uses the Meta Pixel and Meta’s Conversions API. They tell Meta about actions such as viewing a perfume, adding to cart, starting an order, sending an order (counted as a purchase). This uses cookies (for example _fbp and _fbc), your browser details and IP address, and the advertising link you arrived from (such as utm tags or the Facebook click ID).

When you place an order, we may also send Meta your phone number, email, first and last name, city and country in hashed (scrambled, one-way coded) form, so Meta can match the order to an ad. If you ordered before on this device, the same hashed details (saved only on your phone) are also used when you view or add a perfume. Your address is never sent to Meta. The purchase is reported when you place the order.

We also send these same actions from our own server (Conversions API), so an ad blocker does not hide them. Each action has one ID, so Meta counts it only once.

Meta’s own use of this information is covered by Meta’s Privacy Policy.

Ads measurement on this device: On
If you turn it off, this website will not load the Meta Pixel on this device and will not send your actions or orders to Meta from our server.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them for our business and accounts. You can ask us to delete your order details at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp, unless we must keep them for a legal reason.

Your choices

  • Email is optional. You can order without it.
  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • You can turn off ads measurement on this device with the switch above.

We do not sell your personal information.
