Payment

Payment options at checkout

On the website customers can tick "Pay the full amount now" and get this discount on the perfumes (not on delivery). The server calculates it. The advance is not shown on the website; you ask for it on WhatsApp. The advance amount below is only used inside Admin.

Delivery charge

Added to every website order. The 1% full-payment discount is never taken off the delivery charge.

"Free delivery from" uses the perfume total before the discount. Put 0 to never give free delivery.

QR codes and account details

When a customer ticks full payment, the cart shows the QR codes that are ON, with the account details. They can tap a QR to make it big and save it.

Import / Update CSV

Import into

Importing into PERFUME. Needs a name column (Perfume Name, Attar Name or Name) and size columns like 3ml, 6ml, 10ml, 15ml, 30ml. Optional: House, Gender (Male / Female / Unisex), Season (Summer / Winter / All season), Similar To, Fragrantica, SL (number), Category (), Juice. Matching products are updated, new ones are added, nothing is deleted, and photos are kept. Blank cells change nothing.
