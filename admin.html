Import into 

Importing into PERFUME. Needs a name column (Perfume Name, Attar Name or Name) and size columns like 3ml, 6ml, 10ml, 15ml, 30ml. Optional: SL (number), Brand, Category (), Juice. Matching products are updated, new ones are added, nothing is deleted, and photos are kept. Blank cells change nothing.
